### [🔒 LOCKED] ADR-091: DNK OS Agentic Microkernel Architecture

- **Subject:** Agentic Microkernel Architecture, 4-Layer Separation, and Pydantic SSOT Contracts
- **Status:** `[🔒 LOCKED]` (ADR-091)
